# IF-234 — date_format writes the century of a year before 0000 as characters that are not a number

## Evidence and boundary

Found on 2026-11-18 in the recording taken for IF-231 stage 4. `%C` is the
year divided by 100, rounded down. For a year before 0000 that is negative,
and `chrono` wrote it by turning the number into a byte and then into two
characters: the year -0001 gave `I5`, the year -9999 gave `?6`. Its
documentation says `-1`.

Reproduced before the fix through the node: input `-0001-12-31` with
`input_format = "%Y-%m-%d"` and `output_format = "%C"` returned `I5`, with
every padding modifier. `%y` was correct (`99`). Years from 0000 on were
never affected. A year above 9999 had the same fault and is now outside the
range the node accepts (IF-231).

Exposure: cosmetic. A year before 0000 reaches the node only through an
`input_format` with a signed `%Y`, through `%s`, or through a `timezone`
that moves the first hours of the year 0000 back.

## Outcome and validation

`%C` is written as a number in at least two characters: `-1` for the years
-0100 to -0001, `-100` for -9999, `00` for the year 0000. `%-C` and `%_C`
write the same digits without the zero padding. Nothing else changes.

Regression: `date_format_century_of_a_year_before_zero_is_a_number` in
`tests/test_date_node.rs` failed before the fix (`I5|I5|I5|I5|99` instead of
`-1|-1|-1|-1|99`). The golden test names the 22 recorded lines this
corrects and compares them with the corrected text. Validation is recorded
with IF-231 stage 4.
