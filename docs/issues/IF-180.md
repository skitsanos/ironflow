# IF-180 — Numeric cron weekdays fire one day early

## Evidence and boundary

Review of `develop` at 1.21.1-dev.1 on 2026-11-12 (a Thursday).

- Reproduced against a local `ironflow serve` with two schedules: `cron: "* *
  * * 4"` and `cron: "* * * * 5"`. In standard cron, 4 is Thursday and 5 is
  Friday. Only the `5` schedule started runs; the `4` schedule never fired.
- `parse_cron` (`src/scheduler/cron.rs:55-68`) prepends a seconds field and
  passes the remaining fields unchanged to `cron::Schedule::from_str`. In
  `cron` 0.17.0 the day-of-week field is 1 to 7 with Sunday as 1
  (`time_unit/days_of_week.rs`), and matching uses
  `weekday().number_from_sunday()` (`schedule.rs:85`).
- Consequences:
  - `0 9 * * 1-5` runs Sunday to Thursday, not Monday to Friday;
  - `0 0 * * 0` is rejected at startup as not valid;
  - `0 0 * * 7` runs on Saturday.
- Documentation calls the syntax "standard five-field cron"
  (`docs/CLI_REFERENCE.md`, schedules section; `README.md`;
  `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`). The only weekday regression uses the name `Fri`
  (`tests/test_schedule_config.rs`), which the crate maps correctly, so
  numeric weekdays are untested. IF-074's own example `0 4 1 * 5` therefore
  means Thursday today.

Boundary: weekday names are correct. Day-of-month, month and the
day-or-weekday union were not re-tested here.

## Required outcome and acceptance

- Normalize the weekday field before handing it to the crate: `0` and `7`
  are Sunday, `1` is Monday, through `6` for Saturday, including ranges,
  lists and steps. Reject anything outside 0 to 7 with a clear message.
- Regressions with a fixed clock for `1-5`, `0`, `7`, `6,0`, `*/2` and a
  day-of-month plus weekday union, asserting the fired calendar days.
- State the accepted weekday numbering in `docs/CLI_REFERENCE.md`, `README.md`
  and the schedule example, and add an upgrade note: schedules written with
  numeric weekdays will move by one day.
- Evidence: focused scheduler tests and the schedule examples validated.

## Outcome and validation

Resolved on 2026-11-12.

- Cause confirmed as filed: `parse_cron` handed the weekday field to the
  `cron` crate unchanged, and the crate numbers the week 1 to 7 from Sunday.
- `src/scheduler/weekday.rs` (new) validates the weekday field and rewrites
  it before `parse_cron` builds the crate expression, for the single schedule
  and for the weekday half of the day-of-month union alike. Numbers, ranges
  (`N-M`), stepped ranges (`N-M/S`) and lists are expanded to the crate's
  numbering with 0 and 7 as Sunday. `*`, `?` and their steps pass through:
  a step over `*` starts on Sunday in both numberings, so `*/2` was already
  Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and is unchanged. Names pass through to
  the crate.
- Rejected with a one-line message that names the weekday field: a number
  outside 0 to 7, a backwards range (`6-1`), a step outside 1 to 7, an empty
  list item, a range that mixes a name and a number, and stray characters.

Contract boundary:

- `N/S` is not standard crontab. It is kept and defined as `N` through `7`
  (`1/2` is Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday).
- Named ranges still follow the crate's Sunday-first order: `Sun-Sat` is
  accepted and `Sat-Sun` is rejected at startup. Not changed here.
- The weekday field no longer accepts trailing characters that the crate read
  as a year (`Mon2030` was Mondays in 2030). The crate also accepts adjacent
  fields without a space elsewhere: `5*/2 * * * *` is still read as minute 5
  of every second hour. That separate defect was fixed afterwards as
  [IF-206](IF-206.md).
- Schedules written with numeric weekdays move by one day on upgrade; see the
  upgrade note in `docs/releases/next.md`. Stored claims are keyed by
  wall-clock time and need no migration.

Regressions: `tests/test_schedule_weekdays.rs` (19 tests, fixed clock, the
fortnight from Sunday 2026-11-08) asserts the fired calendar days for `1-5`,
`0`, `7`, `6,0`, `*/2`, `*/3`, `5-7`, `0-7`, `1-5/2`, `1/2`, every number
0 to 7, the `* * * * 4` and `* * * * 5` reproduction, `0 4 1 * 5` and two
more day-of-month unions, names, the weekday in a non-UTC schedule zone, and
the rejections. `src/scheduler/weekday.rs` has three unit tests.

Validation on the final tree (focused, default features):

- Before the fix (the same test file with the rewrite bypassed): 16 of the
  19 tests failed. `0 9 * * 1-5` fired Sunday to
  Thursday, `0 0 * * 7` on Saturdays, `0 0 * * 0` and `6,0` were refused, and
  `* * * * 4` next fired on Wednesday 2026-11-18. The three that passed were
  `*/2`, the unrestricted field, and unknown names staying rejected.
- After the fix: `cargo test --test scheduler` 92 passed; `--test cli` 92;
  `--test examples` 11; `--test serial -- --test-threads=1` 186;
  `--test scheduler_admission` 1; `cargo test --lib` 605 passed, 1 ignored.
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `python3 -B scripts/check_module_size.py`
  and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` passed.
- `ironflow validate examples/21-schedules/scheduled_hello.lua` passed.
- Live, local `ironflow serve` (debug build, SQLite store) on Thursday
  2026-11-12 UTC, four minutely schedules for 95 seconds: `4` and `1-5` each
  started three runs, `5` and `0` started none, and `0` no longer stopped
  startup. `examples/21-schedules/ironflow.yaml` also started and fired its
  minutely schedule.
- Not run: the full integration gate, feature-enabled builds, and a deployed
  instance.
