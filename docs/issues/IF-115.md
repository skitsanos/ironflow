# IF-115 — PPTX extraction uses filenames instead of presentation order and membership

## Required outcome and acceptance

- Read presentation.xml's slide list and relationships to determine membership and reading order; do not infer presentation order from part filenames.
- Resolve associated notes through relationships while enforcing package path/entry limits.
- Test reordered slides, renamed/non-contiguous parts, orphan slide parts, notes, and missing/invalid relationships.

## Evidence and cause

Fresh review on 2026-09-12, finding R10. See
[AUDIT_2026-09-12.md](./AUDIT_2026-09-12.md) for the reviewed candidate,
evidence boundaries, and complete finding-to-issue mapping.

[Source: pptx_parser/package.rs:21](../../src/nodes/extract/pptx_parser/package.rs).

The implementation enumerates and numerically sorts every `slideN.xml`, ignoring `presentation.xml`'s slide list and relationships. A reordered fixture returned the slides in the wrong order and included a third orphan part that was not in the presentation. This directly affects page-by-page reading and RAG provenance. Follow the presentation relationship order and resolve notes through relationships as well. Evidence: extraction probe `reordered`.

## Outcome and validation

Resolved on 2026-09-13. `presentation.xml`'s direct slide list and its
relationships now determine slide membership and order. `slide_index` is the
one-based presentation position in every format and in both comment outputs.
Listed hidden slides remain included; orphan slides, notes, comments and author
parts are not extracted.

- A bounded presentation graph validates slide references before content/media
  extraction. Missing manifests or referenced parts, duplicate IDs or normalized
  slide targets, incorrect slide relationship types, external structural
  targets, and ambiguous associated-part relationships fail without a filename
  fallback.
- Notes and legacy comments use the referring slide's actual relationship part;
  author lists use the presentation relationship. Images resolve against the
  actual source slide directory, including renamed and non-contiguous parts.
- Shared package-path resolution accepts relative and package-root-relative
  targets, rejects root escapes and unsafe URI/path forms, and preserves literal
  case, Unicode and percent-looking sequences. It never opens external targets.
  Presentation/relationship parsing uses XML-only decoding, namespace-aware
  membership, existing item/output/ZIP budgets, CRC validation, and cooperative
  checkpoints. Presentation nesting is capped at 128 elements.
- Existing synthetic decks now contain explicit manifests. The deterministic
  repeated-media benchmark generator and fixture were updated together with
  their checksum. The bundled example fixture bytes did not change. Node docs,
  benchmark docs and the Lua example describe and exercise the new contract.

Pre-fix evidence: the initial three `test_pptx_presentation` regressions failed
against the prior implementation. The reordered fixture returned three slides
instead of two, a renamed slide outside `ppt/slides/slideN.xml` was missed, and a
deck without a presentation manifest was accepted. All three now pass.

Boundary: this is structural extraction, not slide rendering, modern-comment
support, or complete OOXML schema validation. Orphan ZIP headers still count
toward archive-wide admission limits. Already published artifacts are not
rolled back after later content or output failures. The full integration gate,
feature-enabled storage services, release benchmarking, publication and release
remain separate from this focused local fix.
