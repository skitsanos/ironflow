# IF-224 — extract_pptx does not tell hidden slides apart

## Evidence and boundary

Recorded on 2026-11-16 from the IF-222 sample run. A deck saved by
PowerPoint 16 had 13 of its 86 slides hidden (`show="0"` on the `p:sld`
root). `extract_pptx` extracted them like any other slide, in every format,
so a consumer could not skip material the author had switched off.
Reproduced by a failing regression on a hand-built deck before the fix.

## Outcome and validation

A hidden slide's JSON object carries `"hidden": true`; a shown slide has no
`hidden` field, like the other optional slide fields. `show="0"` and
`show="false"` hide a slide; `1`, `true` and an absent attribute do not. A
`show` attribute below the root is ignored.

Contract boundary:

- Hidden slides are still extracted, keep their `slide_index` and are
  counted in `slide_count`.
- Text and Markdown are unchanged and do not mark hidden slides. Whether
  they should, or whether a parameter should drop hidden slides, was not
  asked for and is not decided.
- The flat comments array is unchanged.

Regression: `tests/test_pptx_hidden.rs` (two tests) in the `documents`
binary, failing before the change with no flag on any slide. Documented in
`docs/nodes/extract_pptx.md`, the example and the release notes.

Validation, default features: `documents` 218 passed (3 ignored),
`examples` 11, the 38 extraction tests of `serial`, formatting, the module-size check and
`cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`. Not run: the integration gate
and feature-enabled suites.
