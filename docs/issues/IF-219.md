# IF-219 — Diagnostic scrubber over-redacts and misses some key forms

## Evidence and boundary

Recorded on 2026-11-13 from the fixes for IF-176 and IF-177.

- (a) Measured: scrubbing a diagnostic twice over-redacts. A `key: value`
  ends at a line break, the scrubber turns the break into a space, and the
  second pass (node, then engine) takes the following lines as the value. On
  one line, a `Header: value` credential also removes ordinary words up to
  the next comma. Over-redaction only.
- (b) By reading: the scrubber does not match a Lua bracket key
  (`["password"] = "x"`) or `key => value`.
- (c) Decision needed: a sender who controls a forwarded header can still
  blank a string of eight or more bytes that a later step outputs (IF-176,
  IF-011). Closing it means choosing record-only scrubbing or failing the
  step.

Boundary: (a) and (c) lose information but leak nothing; (b) can leave a
secret in a diagnostic when a tool prints it in one of those forms.

## Required outcome and acceptance

- (a) Make a second pass a no-op on already scrubbed text, or scrub once at
  one boundary.
- (b) Match the two forms, with tests.
- (c) Record the decision and implement it.
