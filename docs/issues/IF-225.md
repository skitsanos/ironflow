# IF-225 — Verify extract_word against a document saved by Word or LibreOffice

## Evidence and boundary

Split from IF-222 (f) on 2026-11-16. Nothing here is a reproduced defect:
it is verification that could not be done.

The DOCX behaviour fixed under IF-193 and IF-222 is covered by hand-built
fixtures and by two tool-authored files (pandoc and `textutil`). The one
real document available in the sample run of 2026-11-16 was written by a
generator library, not by an office suite: it has no text box, no comment
on a range, no tracked change, no header, footer or footnote text. So the
structures those fixes handle have never been compared with what Word or
LibreOffice actually writes.

## Required outcome and acceptance

Needs a `.docx` saved by Word or LibreOffice that contains, with made-up
text:

- a text box (`mc:AlternateContent` with `wps:txbx` and the VML fallback),
- a comment anchored on a range, ideally one around or inside the text box,
- a tracked formatting change (`w:rPrChange`) and a tracked insertion and
  deletion,
- a header, a footer and a footnote,
- bold or italic that comes only from a paragraph or character style.

Acceptance: run `extract_word` in each format and compare with the package
XML; record the structural facts here; fix each defect found with a
hand-built fixture and a failing-first regression. If the file may be
committed, add it under `examples/fixtures/` and to
`test_tool_authored_documents`. Close when the comparison is recorded, with
or without defects.
