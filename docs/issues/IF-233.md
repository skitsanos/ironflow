# IF-233 — extract_xlsx fails the step on a date or elapsed-time cell with an extreme value

## Evidence and boundary

Found on 2026-11-18 while recording date cells for IF-231 stage 4. A cell
with a date or elapsed-time number format was converted through `calamine`'s
`chrono` helpers, which turn the serial into a count of milliseconds and
panic when that count is below what `chrono` accepts. Cell text is
untrusted and `-1e300` and `-inf` are valid numbers in a worksheet.

Reproduced before the fix: a workbook with one date-formatted cell holding
`-1e300`, `-inf` or `-106751991168` failed the whole step with `blocking
step worker failed: task panicked with message "TimeDelta::milliseconds out
of bounds"`, in both date systems, and the same for an elapsed-time cell.
The panic stayed inside the blocking worker; the process was not affected.

Exposure: low. One crafted or damaged cell makes a workbook unreadable; no
memory is exposed and nothing is written.

## Outcome and validation

`src/nodes/extract/xlsx/cells.rs` converts serials itself on `jiff` types, and
`calamine`'s `dates` feature is off, which also removes that path to `chrono`
(IF-231). A value that is not a number, or that does not fit 64-bit
milliseconds, is `null`, like an Excel error cell.

Differences, each recorded in `tests/test_extract_xlsx_datetimes.rs`:

- A date after the year 9999 was written with a sign (`+10000-01-01T00:00:00`,
  up to the year 262142) and is `null`. `jiff` holds the years -9999 to 9999,
  and Excel shows no date after 9999-12-31.
- `NaN` was read as day zero (`1899-12-30T00:00:00`, or `PT0S` for an elapsed
  time) and is `null`.
- An elapsed time of 2^63 milliseconds or more was written as the largest
  count (`PT2562047788015H12M55.807S`) and is `null`.

Every other recorded value is unchanged: 34 date serials in both date systems
(the invented 1900-02-29, rounding at a second and at a day, years before
0000) and the elapsed-time values on both sides of the limit. One `jiff`
behaviour was found on the way: adding a duration to a date fails when the
duration alone is longer than 1970 to 9999, wherever the sum lands, so dates
are counted from 1970.

Regression: `a_serial_no_calendar_holds_is_null_not_a_failed_step` failed
before the fix with the panic above. Validation is recorded with IF-231
stage 4, which ran in the same change set.
