# IF-132 — A continuously progressing artifact download delays cancellation until EOF

## Required outcome and acceptance

- Observe cancellation while chunks keep arriving by checking per chunk or retaining a stable polling deadline.
- Stop and clean staging promptly after timeout without waiting for remote EOF; retain admission ownership until physical work stops.
- Use a bounded loopback stream whose chunks arrive faster than the polling interval, plus a successful integrity-checked transfer and temporary-file cleanup checks.

## Evidence and cause

Fresh review on 2026-09-12, finding R27. See
[AUDIT_2026-09-12.md](./AUDIT_2026-09-12.md) for the reviewed candidate,
evidence boundaries, and complete finding-to-issue mapping.

[Source: artifacts/remote.rs:200](../../src/artifacts/remote.rs).

The 100 ms cancellation timer is recreated for each body chunk. Chunks arriving every 20 ms keep winning, and no checkpoint runs in the body path. A 0.5-second timed-out restore still received and staged all 128 KiB over about 3.5 seconds; the tracked worker held workflow drain until completion. Check cancellation per chunk or retain a stable polling deadline. Evidence: `artifact-cancel/result.json` and `events.json` in the artifact evidence directory. This is a bounded loopback transfer, not a load test.
