# IF-257 — Invalid timezone components are silently normalized to another offset

## Evidence and boundary

At `ae1f686`, `date_format` parsed hours and minutes as independently signed integers and checked only their combined offset. With input `2026-01-01T00:00:00Z`, the CLI accepted `timezone="+01:75"` and returned `2026-01-01 02:15:00 +02:15`. It also accepted embedded signs such as `+01:-30`. This affected the output timezone, not timestamp input parsing.

## Required outcome and acceptance

Validate digits, component widths and minute range before converting. Test malformed minute values, embedded signs and invalid widths; keep UTC, Z, positive and negative half-hour offsets, and the documented less-than-a-day boundary.

## Outcome and validation

The output timezone accepts `UTC`, `Z`, or an ASCII offset with exactly two hour digits (`00–23`) and two minute digits (`00–59`) separated by a colon and preceded by a single sign. Invalid values fail the step instead of changing the displayed time. Regressions cover malformed components and confirm that half-hour offsets and both `23:59` boundaries change the local date and time as appropriate without changing the Unix timestamp.
