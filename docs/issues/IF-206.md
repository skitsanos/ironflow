# IF-206 — A five-token cron expression is accepted with its fields shifted

## Evidence and boundary

Found on 2026-11-12 while probing the IF-180 fix, on that change's tree
(1.21.1-dev.2).

- Reproduced through `parse_cron` with a fixed clock. Each is accepted:
  - `5*/2 * * * *` fires at minute 5 of every second hour;
  - `0 2 * 1* *` fires at 02:00 daily in January only (`0 2 * 1* */2`: in
    even years only);
  - `1 -5 1* * *` fires in minutes 1 to 5 of hour 1;
  - `/5 1* * * *` yields an instant every five seconds of minute 1.

  In all, 19 malformed expressions were accepted, also by
  `ScheduleConfig::new` and the YAML form.
- Cause, by reading `cron` 0.17.0 (`parsing.rs`): fields are separated by
  optional whitespace, a seventh year field is optional, and numbers and
  names skip surrounding whitespace. `parse_cron` required five tokens,
  prepended `0` for seconds and passed the first four through unchecked, so
  one token could be read as two fields, or join its neighbour or the
  seconds field.
- IF-180 already validates the weekday token.

Boundary: not run against a live `serve` before the fix. By reading only,
claims are keyed per minute (`claim_key`), so the five-second reading would
start at most one run a minute. An operator has to mistype a field; nothing
remote triggers this.

## Required outcome and acceptance

- Accept a token only when it is exactly one field; reject anything else at
  startup with one line naming the field and quoting the expression.
- Well-formed expressions fire at the same instants; the day-of-month or
  weekday union is unchanged.
- Failing-first regressions in the `scheduler` binary.

## Outcome and validation

Resolved on 2026-11-12.

- `src/scheduler/field_shape.rs` (new) checks the minute, hour, day-of-month
  and month tokens before `parse_cron` builds the crate expression. An item
  of the comma-separated list is `*`, `?` (day of month only), a number or a
  numeric range, optionally with `/step`, or in the month field a name or a
  named range.
- Anything else is rejected, the first malformed field first: `cron
  expression '5*/2 * * * *' is not valid: minute '5*/2' is not a single
  field; write *, a number or a range, optionally with /step, and separate
  fields with spaces`.
- Documented in `docs/CLI_REFERENCE.md` ("Field syntax"),
  `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the example comments and an upgrade note in
  `docs/releases/next.md`.

Contract boundary: shape only. Values, backwards ranges, step size and month
names are still checked by the crate, and those rejections carry no field
name. No expression the crate read as five fields changed meaning. A
configuration that loaded with a shifted reading now fails startup.

Regressions: `tests/test_schedule_field_shapes.rs` (17 tests) and five unit
tests in `field_shape.rs`.

Validation on the final tree (focused, default features):

- Before the fix: 11 of the 17 tests failed, listing the 19 accepted
  expressions; the 6 that passed pin well-formed lists, ranges, steps, month
  names, the union and out-of-range values.
- After: `cargo test --test scheduler` 109 passed; `--test cli` 92;
  `--test examples` 11; `--test serial -- --test-threads=1` 186;
  `--test scheduler_admission` 1; `cargo test --lib` 610 passed, 1 ignored.
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `python3 -B scripts/check_module_size.py`
  and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` passed.
- Local `ironflow serve` (debug build) exited 1 with that message for
  `5*/2 * * * *`, `0 2 * 1* *` and `/5 1* * * *`.
- Not run: the full integration gate, feature-enabled builds, a deployed
  instance.
